# Community code of conduct

Clotho's community should be a place where people can collaborate safely,
directly, and with intellectual generosity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
  communication styles.
- Critique ideas and changes with specific evidence; do not attack or belittle
  the person proposing them.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and security-reporting boundaries.
- Make room for correction. Acknowledge mistakes, repair harm, and help others
  learn the repository's norms.
- Treat human and agent-generated contributions by the same quality, safety,
  licensing, and review standards.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or retaliation are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path in `SECURITY.md`,
marking the report as a community-conduct matter. Maintainers may warn, remove
content, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant according to
severity and pattern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical;
maintainers involved in a concern must recuse themselves.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this policy consistently and
may clarify it as the community grows.
